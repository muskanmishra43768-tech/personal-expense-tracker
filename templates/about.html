✨ About Personal Expense Tracker

Your Money. Your Control.

Personal Expense Tracker is designed to make everyday money management simple, organized and understandable. It helps you record expenses, monitor your spending, manage budgets and gain a clearer picture of your financial habits.

Who We Are 💜

A simple approach to better expense management.

📖 About Personal Expense Tracker

Managing everyday expenses can become difficult when small purchases are not recorded or reviewed. Personal Expense Tracker provides one organized place where users can record their spending and understand their financial activity.

Instead of depending on scattered notes or remembering expenses manually, users can maintain their expense records, review their history, analyze categories and manage their monthly budget from a single dashboard.

💡 Our idea: When you understand where your money goes, it becomes easier to decide where it should go.
🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to make personal expense tracking simple and accessible. We want users to build better financial awareness by tracking expenses, reviewing their habits and planning their budgets.
